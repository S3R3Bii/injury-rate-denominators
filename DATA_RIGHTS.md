# Source and publication scope

OSHA makes submitted establishment summary data publicly available for transparency and research. Follow https://www.osha.gov/itadata and its Summary Data Dictionary and ITA Data Users Guide. A submission does not itself establish fault, violation, worker risk or representative population estimates. Source accuracy and reporting coverage remain limitations.

No case-detail data are requested. Raw establishment rows, names, addresses and identity fields remain private even when publicly downloadable. Publish only checked minimal statistical counts and independent original results. Source documentation and third-party files are attributed and linked, not copied into the repository. Original code is MIT licensed. Formal-publication projects including Azure remain private.
